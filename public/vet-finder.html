Before your first visit

  • Bring your adoption papers and any vaccination records from the shelter.
  • Ask about microchip registration, desexing and a parasite prevention plan.
  • Book a check-up within two weeks of bringing your pet home.

Emergency? Call your nearest 24-hour animal hospital straight away.
